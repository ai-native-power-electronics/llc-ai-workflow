# Sanitized LTspice method failure

The underlying LTspice schematic, netlist, raw output, derived parameter files
and third-party model material are not redistributed because their publication
rights and provenance have not been cleared.

`PUBLIC_LTSPICE_FAILURE.json` documents only the execution outcome: the
secondary method exceeded its configured timeout and did not produce the
required measurements. It is not a rebuildable LTspice example and it does not
establish an electrical rejection.
